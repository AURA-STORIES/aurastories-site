Railway story

From the plains to Ooty

The 46 km metre-gauge line links Mettupalayam in the foothills with Coonoor and Udhagamandalam (Ooty). The journey climbs from about 326 m to 2,203 m.

Building the line

Proposed in 1854, the railway entered construction in 1891. The first 27 km to Coonoor opened in 1899. The remaining 19 km reached Ooty in 1908.

What the railway changed

A passenger could travel from the plains into the hills by rail. That connection helped people move between towns and supported the region’s economic development. Today the railway still carries passengers and gives visitors a working example of mountain railway engineering.

How the train climbs

On the steep rack section, a toothed wheel beneath the locomotive engages bars between the running rails. Select “See how it climbs” to follow that wheel as the train moves.
